Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 4, 2026

At CoreEfx AI, we prioritize the confidentiality of your health journey. This policy outlines our commitment to transparency and data integrity within our AI health advisor ecosystem.

1. Information We Collect

We are committed to transparency about the data we gather to provide and improve our AI Health Advisor Service.

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Security: Encrypted credentials including username, email, and password.
  • Biometric Context: Age, gender, and pre-existing conditions used solely for diagnostic accuracy.
  • Symptom/Vital Analytics: Textual data regarding health concerns for AI processing.
Crucial Note: We understand the extremely sensitive nature of your health data. We implement strict security measures and processing protocols to ensure your data is never used for advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

We collect your data to provide a secure and valuable AI Health Advisor experience.

  • Operational Logic: Delivering real-time health guidance.
  • AI Refinement: Improving the accuracy of our medical lemmatization models.
  • Safety Protocols: Identifying high-severity symptoms that require urgent hospital intervention.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. Sharing only occurs under the following strict conditions:

  • Service Reliability: With cloud providers under strict NDA.
  • Legal Compliance: To meet applicable laws or enforceable governmental requests.
  • Referral Assitance: Sharing location with nearby hospitals only when you approve location access.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards to protect your information:

  • End-to-End Encryption: Protecting data in transit and at rest.
  • Granular Access: Limiting internal database access to authorized systems only.
  • Continuous Auditing: Vulnerability assessments to stay ahead of security threats.

5. Your Data Rights

In accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), you have full control over your data:

  • The right to request deletion of your medical history.
  • The right to export your data in machine-readable formats.
  • The right to withdraw consent for location tracking at any time.
